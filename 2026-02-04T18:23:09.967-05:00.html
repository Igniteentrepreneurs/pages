Got Questions?

We've Got Answers

I'm intimidated by gyms. Will I feel out of place?

Not at all. That's exactly why most of our members chose RAF. We're a small, welcoming community—not a crowded gym with people showing off. Everyone here is a busy professional or parent, just like you. You'll feel like you belong from day one.

I haven't exercised in years. Can I really do this?

Yes—most of our members started exactly where you are. Every exercise is modified to your current ability. Our coaches specialize in working with adults 40+ who are returning to fitness. You'll never feel pushed beyond what's safe.

I only have 3 hours a week. Is that enough?

Absolutely. Our program is specifically designed for busy professionals with limited time. Three 1-hour sessions per week is all you need. And with flexible morning, midday, and evening classes, you can fit it around your schedule.

Will this actually help me lose belly fat?

Yes. Strength training is proven to be more effective for fat loss than cardio alone—especially for adults 40+. Combined with the nutritional guidance we provide, most members see noticeable changes within 4-6 weeks.

How quickly will I see results?

Most members notice better energy and sleep within 2 weeks. Clothes start fitting differently around week 4. By week 12, expect real changes: less belly fat, improved strength, better blood work, and a confidence boost you can feel.

Is this worth the investment?

That depends on what your health is worth to you. Our members find the coaching, community, and accountability makes this more effective—and affordable—than expensive personal training or programs they never stick with. Book a free consultation to discuss options.
